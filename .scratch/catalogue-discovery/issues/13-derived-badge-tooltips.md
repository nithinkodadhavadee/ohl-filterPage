# 13: Tooltips for derived badges

**What to build:** Available, Unavailable and Low sample — and any future derived badge — explain themselves when hovered or keyboard-focused, in the grid, table and detail view, so a Patron does not have to guess what the label means.

**Blocked by:** None (can start immediately)

**Status:** needs-triage

- [ ] Each derived badge has a short explanation available on hover and focus.
- [ ] The same badge component and wording are used in grid, table and detail view.
- [ ] Tooltips are keyboard-accessible and not hover-only.
- [ ] Low-sample wording states that the Rating rests on fewer than five contributions.
